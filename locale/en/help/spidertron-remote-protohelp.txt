This is a spidertron remote, used to send one or more spidertrons. It is the game's own remote: get one from the shortcut bar.

To select spidertrons, press left bracket at one corner of an area, move the cursor to the other corner, and press left bracket again. The spidertrons in the area become the selection. Selecting an area with no spidertrons clears the selection.

Shift left bracket the same way adds the spidertrons in the area to the selection. Control left bracket removes them.

Press right bracket to send the selected spidertrons to the cursor. On a building, a vehicle or a character, they follow it instead.

Press shift right bracket to add the cursor as a waypoint after the ones already queued. A waypoint queued close to an earlier one makes the spidertrons patrol between them.

On a spidertron, the cursor says when it is selected, what it follows, and where it walks.
